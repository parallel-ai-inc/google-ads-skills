## What Google documents

These lines are **Official**.

- **Where it serves.** Demand Gen serves visual ads on YouTube (including Shorts), Discover, Gmail, Maps and the Google Display Network. (About Demand Gen campaigns)
- **Display moves in.** Google Display Ads campaigns are moving into Demand Gen. The migration tool rolled out from June 2026 and the move is expected to finish by 2027. A migration carries the settings and up to 42 days of history, and a migrated campaign cannot be turned back into a Display campaign. Google advises not migrating during a lift study. (Display Ads have a new home in Demand Gen)
- **Channels.** Channel controls are set per ad group: YouTube in-stream, in-feed and Shorts, Discover, Gmail, Maps and the Display Network. Google recommends all channels. To push spend into one channel, give it its own ad group with a higher CPA target, at some cost to efficiency. (Channel controls)
- **Settings it does not have.** No frequency cap and no shared budgets. Location and language are set at campaign or ad group level, never both. Brand safety is set at account level through content suitability. (FAQ)
- **Content exclusions are not settled.** Google's pages disagree on whether content exclusions sit at account level or per campaign. Check the account. (FAQ)
- **Learning.** The learning phase lasts up to around 50 conversion events or 3 conversion cycles. (Learning phase)
- **Changes restart learning.** Changing settings makes the campaign redo some or all of its learning, and a large change can amount to a total reset. (Performance guide)
- **Bidding to start.** For an advertiser new to Demand Gen, or without a set target, Google suggests starting on Maximize conversions and moving to Target CPA or Target ROAS after at least 50 conversions. (Performance guide)
- **Budget.** A daily budget of at least 10 times the target CPA. (Performance guide)
- **Changing bids.** Change bids by 5% to 10% a week, never more than 15%. Make no changes during the learning period. Wait for 30 to 50 conversions before judging a change. To optimize for a different conversion goal, start a new campaign rather than change the goal on a live one. (Performance guide)
- **Value based bidding.** No bid or creative changes in the first 14 days. Start Target ROAS about 20% below the campaign's history. (Value based bidding)
- **Creative refresh.** Add new assets before removing old ones. Remove only assets that ran at least 14 days and missed the goal. (Creative refresh)
- **Lookalike segments** work only in Demand Gen and are moving to a suggestion mode through 2026. An advertiser can opt out to keep them as a constraint. (Lookalike segments)
- **Channel report.** The channel performance report covers Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns. (Channel performance report)

## How the reviews use it

These lines are **Practitioner rules**.

- The Demand Gen section of the review prints each Demand Gen campaign's conversions across the file, its conversions by week, and its daily budget against the Target CPA, so the thresholds above can be read off the output.
- Google's Search guidance on targets does not carry over. On Search a target change does not restart learning; on Demand Gen, Google's own guide says a settings change does. Use the page for the campaign type in question.
- The channel split, the content suitability settings and the Lookalike setting are not in any export this skill reads. Check them in the account.
