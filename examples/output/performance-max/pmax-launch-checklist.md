## 5. Exclusions and safety

- [ ] Brand exclusions, if Search campaigns should own brand searches. Official: they block brand terms, misspellings and subsidiary brands on Search and Shopping, and Google prefers them over negatives. Account number: brand searches are 16.4% of the listed Performance Max search cost and 48.4% of the listed conversions in the search terms report.
- [ ] Negative keywords. Official: they apply to Search and Shopping inventory only, up to 10,000 per campaign and 1,000 at account level, with no close variant matching.
- Account number: words in two or more current searches with cost and no conversions, to review as negatives: "jobs" (265.00), "diy" (195.00).
- Held back, never to be added as negatives here: "drain" (475.00, found in the new campaign's name and the asset group names it copies and the titles of the products it will carry); "snake" (400.00, found in the titles of the products it will carry). Every word appears in what this campaign sells or builds, so a negative would block its own business.
- [ ] Brand safety on Display and video. Official: block placements there through excluded content keywords in content suitability.
- [ ] Overlap with Search. Official: only an exact match keyword identical to the search is preferred over Performance Max. Identical phrase or broad keywords share the next priority with identical search themes.

4. Asset groups built and Ad strength checked.
5. Exclusions applied.
6. Enable on 2026-11-20.
7. From 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-22: check it serves. Status, impressions and cost by channel, and any policy issues. Practitioner rule.
8. 2026-12-03: first read of the channel report Status column, asset group Ad strength and the search terms. Official: allow 1 to 2 weeks, or one full conversion cycle, after a significant change, and avoid frequent budget, bid strategy or status changes early.
9. 2026-12-31: earliest full judgment. Official: run a new campaign at least 6 weeks. Only about half of Performance Max conversions land inside the 30 day click and 3 day engaged view windows, so early reads understate it.
10. Compare the copy with the source's numbers above, and with its channel mix: Google Search and Search partners carry 58.2% of its cost in the channel report. Practitioner rule.
