# Northside Plumbing: Google Ads, October 2026

Monthly business review, prepared by Harbor Search Partners on November 13, 2026.

_Reporting period: October 1, 2026 to October 31, 2026. Compared with September 1, 2026 to September 30, 2026 and with October 1, 2025 to October 31, 2025._

## Month-over-month snapshot

October brought 413.49 conversions at a cost of 14,423.32, against 407.22 conversions in September. Conversions rose 1.5% while cost fell 3.8%.

Without Brand, cost per conversion was 50.24, which is 25.6% above the 40.00 target. Brand searches convert cheaply by nature, so the whole account's figure of 34.88 looks better than the rest of the account performs.

- Against October last year, conversions rose 17.5%.
- Spend came in 9.9% below the spend target of 16,000.00, mostly because Drain Cleaning | Search was paused.

## Main drivers

Plumbing | Water Heaters was the biggest mover. Its conversions fell 34.1% after its bid strategy moved to Target CPA on October 6, 2026, with the target set at 45.00. That change is the first lead to check, not proven cause.

Plumbing | Emergency grew. Its conversions rose 19.3% after its daily budget rose from 200.00 to 240.00 on October 1, 2026. Google's systems also added 6 keywords to it on October 14, 2026.

- Drain Cleaning | Search was paused on October 20, 2026 and its last day with cost was October 19, 2026, so its cost fell by 1,410.48. It did not decline; it stopped.

## Budget shifts

Plumbing | Emergency took 58.1% of October's cost and brought 50.2% of its conversions. The pause of Drain Cleaning | Search freed budget that was not moved elsewhere.

## Recommended next actions

- Review the Target CPA on Plumbing | Water Heaters. It was raised after the month closed, so November will show whether that helps.
- Decide with you whether Drain Cleaning | Search comes back on, and on what budget.
- Judge cost per conversion without Brand from now on, since Brand hides the gap to target.

## About these numbers

### How the numbers were made

- This report covers October 1, 2026 to October 31, 2026 (31 days) and compares it with September 1, 2026 to September 30, 2026 (30 days) and with the same period last year, October 1, 2025 to October 31, 2025.
- All figures come from the Google Ads campaign report for those dates. Amounts are in USD.
- A change is the difference between the periods divided by the earlier period. Cost per conversion and return on ad spend (conv. value / cost) are worked out from the period totals.
- The periods differ in length, so per day figures are shown too.
- Account without brand leaves out: Brand.
- Brand campaigns ran at a cost per conversion of 4.13, against 50.24 for the account without brand, and a return on ad spend of 72.29, against 6.49 without brand. Searches for a business's own name convert cheaply by nature, so the figures without brand show how the rest of the account performs.
- Targets are the ones you gave for this period.
- Account changes come from the account's change history.

### What these numbers cannot show

- Conversions for the last days of the period can still rise as late conversions are reported.
- Conversions are counted under the account's conversion and attribution settings. They are not the same as sales or leads in your own systems.
- Nothing outside Google Ads: the website, other channels, or what happened after a lead came in.
- A change made in the period shows timing, not proof of cause.
- Campaign status is as of the day the data was downloaded.
